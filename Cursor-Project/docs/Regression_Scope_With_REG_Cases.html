How the rank is chosen

Ask these in order. The first yes fixes the rank. A missing test does not raise the rank. This rank is not the Jira field Board 1, Board 1.1, or Board 1.2.

  1. If this case fails, does posted money change, or does who is billed or who has supply change? HIGHEST
  2. It does not post the amount itself, but it is the entrance to that chain or a neighboring process. HIGH
  3. It is catalog, a read, a message, or customer master data, and none of those create an amount. MEDIUM
  4. What remains is a screen, a group that does not block contract create, or a field format. SCREENS

Posted money is an invoice amount, a liability, a receivable, an offset, a cancellation or reversal that restores those balances, a late-payment fine, a deposit liability, or a manual offset. Who is billed is a signed contract plus an active point of delivery. Who has supply is the disconnection flag, the disconnection fee, and reconnection clearing that flag.

Highest chain, left to right: who is billed, what is billed, posted debt, debt rewritten, supply. Customer email for that document is High. The amount stays Highest.
