Reproduce Steps:
For Liabilities (from LPF) and Receivables generated from EasyPay payments, the system should follow the same accounting period rules as for all other payments.
If the payment date falls within a previous accounting period and that period is still open, the Liability/Receivable should be created in that accounting period. If the accounting period is closed, the Liability/Receivable should be created in the next currently open accounting period.

GE Description: 
CHANGE TITLE

Assign Accounting period on Customer Payment, Customer Receivable, and LPF Customer Liability using the occurrence-/payment-date period when that period is open; otherwise use the current accounting period
BUSINESS CONTEXT

During EasyPay / online payment confirm, Phoenix can create a Customer Payment, a Customer Receivable (when amount remains / overpayment cases), and — when a late fine applies — a Late Payment Fine that generates a new Customer Liability (LPF liability). Each of these three objects stores Accounting period.
REQUESTED CHANGE (TO-BE)

When EasyPay / online payment creates any in-scope object, the system must:
Determine the driving date for that object.

Identify the accounting period respective for that date.

If that period is open at creation → assign that period.

If that period is closed (or does not exist) → assign the current accounting period.

SCOPE

In scope:
Customer Payment created by EasyPay / online payment

Customer Receivable created by EasyPay / online payment

Customer Liability created from Late Payment Fine during EasyPay / online payment (LPF liability)

In-scope objects and driving dates
Customer Payment → Payment date

Customer Receivable → Occurrence date (online: first / check request date)

Customer Liability from LPF → Occurrence date (online: check request date)
