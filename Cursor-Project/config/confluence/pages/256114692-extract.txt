When the month (with year) of Invoice Period To is the same as the month (with year) of Deactivation date, With electricity price components are invoiced for this POD. Contract-level With electricity price components are calculated when at least one POD on the contract has an Activation date and its Deactivation date (when populated) is greater than or equal to the invoiced month (month with year).
