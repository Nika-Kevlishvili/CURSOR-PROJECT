Most frequently used Conditions of price component: 

1 Customer type, example: the excise duty for commercial customers is 2 BGN/MWh, and 0 for households, so two different price components should be created with the corresponding condition. Some of the grid prices are also differentiated by customer type (access to the distribution grid)
2 POD grid operator, examples: all grid prices for access and for distribution are different for the objects connected to the different grid operators
3 Type of POD, example: for products that include POD from both types (consumer and generator) the prices for them are different.
4 POD voltage level, examples: the grid prices for distribution are different depending on the voltage of the PoD
5 Contract type, example: for the products where the user can select between combined or other type of contract, all grid prices should be applied only when combined is selected

Examples of penalties

Example 1 (product Home):

Upon termination of the Contract in the cases under Art. 19, para. 1, letter "c", which occurred before the expiration of the term under Art. 4, para. 2, the PARTY that caused the termination owes the other PARTY a penalty in the amount of 0.55 BGN per day for the period from the date of termination to the end of the term of the Agreement according to Art. 4, para. 2, but for no more than 360 (three hundred and sixty) days.

 

Example 2 (product with fixed price for mass market 2024):

In case of premature termination of the Contract, the PARTY that caused the termination owes the other PARTY a penalty in the amount of 50.00 BGN for every point of delivery under this Contract for each unfulfilled month from the date of termination to the end of the agreed term under Art. 4, para. 2 of this Contract but not less than 100.00 BGN.

 

Example 3 (product Market Trend):

Upon termination of the Contract under Art. 18, para. 1, letter "b", in case the confirmation from the grid operator was received after the start of delivery for the relevant points of delivery under Annex No. 1 and before the expiration of the period under Art. 4, para.  2, the BUYER owes a penalty to the SELLER and COORDINATOR for the days from the date of termination of the Contract until the agreed end of the Contract, but for no more than 360 (three hundred and sixty) days, in the amount of 10% (ten percent) of the price according to the latest invoice issued by the SELLER and COORDINATOR under Art. 12 of the Contract multiplied by the average daily consumption of the BUYER, calculated according to the amounts of net electricity invoiced up to the date of termination for the relevant points of delivery, but not less than 50 BGN.

 

 

Example 4 (product Save):

Upon termination of the Contract under Art. 18, para. 1, letter "g", in case the notice of termination is given after the start of delivery for the relevant points of delivery under Annex No. 1 and before the expiry of the period under Art. 4, para. 2, the PARTY that gave the notice owes a penalty to the other PARTY for the days from the date of termination of the Contract to the end of the Contract term, but for no more than 360 (three hundred and sixty) days, in the amount of 10% (ten percent) of the agreed price multiplied by the average daily consumption of the BUYER, calculated according to the consumption invoiced up to the date of termination for the relevant points of delivery, but not less than 50 BGN.

 

Example 5 (product Fix 1):

If the Contract is terminated by Art 17 Para 1, Letter “f”, if the termination notice has been sent before the supply to the sites of Appendix No. 1 has started, the Party that issued the notice shall owe the other Party for each terminated point of delivery a compensation in the amount of 50% (fifty percent) of the total forecast quantity of electrical energy for the period for the respective point of delivery, by the price negotiated in Appendix No. 2.

 

 

Example 6 (product Fix 1):

In case of non-fulfilment of any contractual obligation with exception of the obligation to pay, for each case of non-fulfilment, the defaulting Party shall owe the other Party compensation in the amount of 50% (fifty percent) of the negotiated forecast average monthly quantity of electrical energy, by the price negotiated in Appendix No. 2.
