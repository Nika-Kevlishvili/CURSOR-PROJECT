Customer logs in and navigates to the “Invoices and Payments” section, where the list of liabilities is displayed. At this step the portal will receive the IDs of all liabilities in the list. When the user clicks on a liability, the Portal sends the GET request with the ID provided in the first step and upon receiving the response, displays more detailed information and an option to pay it.
